Step 1Brainstorming
Think of something good that you did, you usually do, or you want to do.
Write down information that will make your speech more persuasive.

Step 2Make an outline.
Decide on your message and fill in the outline. Organize your ideas into 3 or 4 paragraphs.
Introduction(your action) 聴衆を話題に惹きつける工夫
Body(explanation of your action) 具体例をあげながら説明
Conclusion(your message) 伝えたい内容をまとめる
Step 3Write it down.
Write the script for your speech. Write at least 200 words.
段落は空行で区切りましょう（Introduction / Body / Conclusion）。
